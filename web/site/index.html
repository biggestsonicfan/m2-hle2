          Advanced: CRT filter and shaders

          These filters draw the picture a second time on your graphics chip, every frame, and can cut performance. If the game slows down or stutters, set the filter back to None.

          Filters cover only the game's picture. They read it at the size chosen under Feels laggy? → Picture; for your own shader, “Full resolution” means the arcade's 496×384.

          Your own shader

          A libretro GLSL shader: a .glslp preset with the .glsl files and textures it uses, or a single .glsl, from libretro's glsl-shaders. Pick the files, or a folder that holds them. Slang presets (.slangp) don't work in a browser.
